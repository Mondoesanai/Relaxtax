What's a Typical Tax Preparation Fee in Plano?

2026-09-24

There's no universal answer to "what does tax preparation cost?" A simple return with one W-2 is a very different job than a small business return with payroll, multiple schedules, and quarterly planning. The honest answer is: it depends on what your return actually needs.

Rather than quote a generic number that may not apply to your situation, we walk through your paperwork first and give you a clear, flat-fee quote before we start any work.

What actually drives the cost

A few things typically make a return more involved — and change what a fair fee looks like:

  • Whether you're filing as an individual, a sole proprietor, or a business entity
  • How many income sources, schedules, or states are involved
  • Whether you need bookkeeping cleaned up before the return can even be prepared
  • Whether you're also asking for tax planning, not just filing
  • How much documentation is organized versus scattered across the year

Two people can both call it "a simple return" and mean very different things once you look at the actual documents.

Flat fee vs. hourly — why it matters

Some preparers bill by the hour, which can make the final cost hard to predict. We prefer flat-fee pricing whenever possible: once we understand your situation, you get a number up front, not a surprise later. That's easier to budget for, whether you're an individual filer or running a small business in Collin County.

Why "cheapest" isn't always the right question

It's natural to search for the lowest possible price, but tax preparation isn't just data entry — it affects your refund, your liability, and how prepared you are if the IRS ever has questions. A fair fee reflects the accuracy and attention your return actually gets, not just the number of forms typed up. If your situation involves a business, payroll, or ongoing bookkeeping, the value of getting it right the first time usually outweighs shopping for the absolute lowest price.

How to get an accurate quote

The most useful thing you can do is skip the guessing and just ask. Bring (or describe) what you're working with — W-2s, 1099s, business income, prior-year returns — and we'll tell you exactly what it will cost before anything is filed. We offer tax preparation, bookkeeping, payroll, tax planning, and IRS representation, so if your needs go beyond a single return, we can quote the full picture at once.
