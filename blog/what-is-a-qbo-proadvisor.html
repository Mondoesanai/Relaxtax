What Is a QBO ProAdvisor?

2026-10-24

A QuickBooks Online (QBO) ProAdvisor is someone who has completed Intuit's training and certification program for the QuickBooks Online platform. The badge tells you that person has studied the software in depth — not that they're automatically a great bookkeeper, but that they know the tool inside and out.

What the Certification Actually Covers

The ProAdvisor program focuses on QuickBooks Online itself: setup, navigation, reporting, bank feeds, and troubleshooting common issues inside the software. It's a technology credential, not a license to practice accounting. Someone can be a skilled bookkeeper without it, and someone can hold the badge without years of hands-on experience with real client books.

Why It Still Matters When You're Hiring

If your business runs on QuickBooks Online, working with someone who's comfortable in the platform can save you time. They're less likely to get stuck on basic features and more likely to set up your chart of accounts, invoicing, and reports in a way that actually makes sense for your business.

That said, the badge is just one data point. What matters more is whether the person or firm understands your industry, communicates clearly, and keeps your books accurate and current — month after month, not just at tax time.

Questions Worth Asking Before You Hire

  • Who will actually be doing the work on my books — the same person every month, or does it rotate?
  • How often will I get updated financials or reports?
  • Does the person handling bookkeeping also understand how it connects to my tax filing?
  • What happens if I have a question between scheduled check-ins?

A good answer to that last question matters more than any badge. Bookkeeping that sits disconnected from your tax picture tends to create surprises down the road.
